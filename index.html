A Konanakunte Institution

South Bengaluru’s Beloved Destination For Family Shopping

Long before commercial chain stores arrived in South Bengaluru, families across Konanakunte, Chunchaghatta, and JP Nagar were already walking into Mahalakshmi Fancy Store. Situated in Preethvi Complex on Chunchaghatta Main Road, our spacious showroom was created with a heartfelt promise: to bring every essential detail of festive preparation, adornment, and home gifting together under one grand roof.

We take pride in old-world hospitality where customers are greeted by name and never rushed. Whether you are searching for Italian bronze kadas that never lose their lustre, matching bindis by the dozen for family ceremonies, evening clutches for a reception, or premium cookware for a housewarming, Mahalakshmi offers the scale of a department store with the warmth of a family shopkeeper.
