A neighbourhood institution on Chunchaghatta Main Road

Long before online shopping came to Konanakunte, families here were already walking into Mahalakshmi Fancy Store for the small things that make a big day feel complete — a matching set of bangles for a wedding, the right bindi for a festival morning, a birthday gift picked out in ten minutes flat.

The shop has built its name the old way: by keeping shelves full of variety, remembering regular customers by face, and never making anyone feel rushed while they choose. It's the kind of store you're pointed to by a neighbour rather than found through an advertisement — which is exactly why it deserves a proper home online.

Everything on these shelves is chosen with one question in mind: will this earn a smile the moment it's unwrapped, worn, or set out on the kitchen counter?
